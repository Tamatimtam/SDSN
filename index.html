Who We Are

Driving sustainable solutions across Indonesia

SDSN Indonesia, hosted by Universitas Indonesia and united in Diversity (UID), Jakarta, was established in 2013. We mobilize our members across Indonesia, leveraging their strengths to help realize the Sustainable Development Goals.

Our key focus areas include addressing knowledge gaps among non-state actors and member institutions through co-organizing workshops, and mobilizing youth to create innovative solutions for societal challenges.
